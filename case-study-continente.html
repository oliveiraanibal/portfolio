Continente Online

Accelerating E-commerce Growth

Leveraging Marketing strategies to boost Acquisition, Conversion, Retention, and Customer Experience for sustained market leadership in a nascent environment.

Context & Contribution

In 2012, the Portuguese e-grocery market was nascent, with online penetration at just 0.9%. Portugal was recovering from a severe economic downturn, leading to subdued consumer confidence. Portuguese consumers became acutely price-sensitive, often shopping at multiple supermarkets for value and promotions without compromising quality, while also prioritizing range and convenience. Retailers, however, struggled with high operational costs and consumer skepticism regarding online product quality, as many users preferred to "see or touch" items. My Contribution:

  • Conducted CX research and produced actionable, data-driven insights.
  • Designed 200+ campaigns across 50+ categories for high-impact artwork and pages.
  • Implemented Growth tactics to scale User Acquisition and Conversion Rates.
  • Partnered on a major website redesign to enhance Customer Experience and sales.

Problem & Opportunity

In 2012, Continente.pt faced significant challenges, including low conversion rate (1.2%) and a substantial 75% cart abandonment rate at checkout. These issues, compounded by the unique characteristics of a nascent and price-sensitive Portuguese e-grocery market, contributed to suboptimal customer lifetime value (CLV) and average order value (AOV).

Despite the challenges, the nascent e-grocery market presented a significant opportunity for Continente.pt, as a pioneer, to define and dominate the online grocery space. By strategically addressing the identified bottlenecks through a strong focus on user experience and data-driven marketing, there was immense potential to capture market share, build customer loyalty, and drive substantial revenue growth in an underdeveloped sector. This was a chance to innovate and set new standards for online grocery shopping in Portugal.

Growth Marketing Initiatives

Optimizing New User Acquisition

New user sign-ups were declining despite higher marketing spend. To understand these barriers we conducted in-depth user interviews and competitor analysis, ensuring our proposed solutions were relevant and competitive.

Our research revealed that lengthy registration and an unclear value proposition deterred sign-ups. We addressed this by implementing social login options and redesigning the landing page to prominently feature clear, upfront discount offers, making the onboarding process quicker and more rewarding.

First impressions and immediate value propositions are critical for successful user acquisition in competitive markets.

Boosting Engagement & AOV

Thematic landing pages experienced inconsistent engagement and Average Order Value (AOV). To improve this, we conducted A/B testing on various design variants, meticulously crafting different layouts, visual styles, and content presentations.

These tests showed simplistic and flat elements with prominent value propositions significantly boosted engagement and AOV. We redesigned the pages with these principles, reducing cognitive load and enhancing visual appeal to encourage deeper engagement and larger purchases on specific campaigns.

Data-driven design iterations, even subtle ones like flat elements, can significantly enhance user interaction and drive commercial results on specific campaigns.

Visual simplicity combined with clear value propositions can dramatically increase engagement and conversion.

Optimizing Seasonal Banner Performance

Seasonal banners had low (12%) click-through despite spend. To understand this, we conducted in-depth heatmap and eye-tracking analysis, precisely pinpointing where users were focusing their attention and how their gaze moved across content, revealing banner ineffectiveness.

Analysis showed 83% of users ignored banners, gravitating towards search and navigation. We repositioned banners closer to these high-attention zones, directly addressing visibility issues and improving performance.

Understanding natural user flow is crucial for effective placement of promotional content while forcing visibility can be counterproductive.

Reducing Checkout Abandonment

A 75% cart abandonment rate was occurring at checkout. To tackle this, we conducted extensive A/B testing on various iterations of the checkout flow, meticulously tracking completion rates, time spent, and error rates across different versions.

Tests unequivocally showed complex forms and ambiguous product substitution options were major friction points. We redesigned the checkout into a simplified 5-step flow with autofill and a clear visual substitution preview, making the experience smoother and more transparent.

Simplifying multi-step processes and providing clear expectations reduces friction, leading to significant revenue recovery and improved user experience.

Transparency and ease of modification within the checkout process are critical for building user trust and preventing last-minute abandonment.

Improving New User Retention

Only 15% of new users became repeat buyers. To address this low retention rate, we conducted a cohort analysis, segmenting users by initial purchase categories and tracking their retention over time to identify patterns.

Analysis revealed customers buying pantry staples had 3x higher retention. We leveraged this by developing personalized 'Pantry Starter' recommendations for new users, promoting these high-retention categories immediately after their first purchase to guide them towards higher long-term engagement.

Facilitating Repeat Purchases

Users abandoned carts when reordering because they struggled to find past items. We performed a detailed feature benchmarking of leading e-commerce platforms and direct competitors, examining their "buy again" features, purchase history displays, and quick-add options.

Benchmarking showed a gap in our platform. We implemented a dedicated 'Buy Again' section with quick-add buttons and smart suggestions based on past order history, effectively reducing friction for loyal customers and making reordering effortless.

Boosting Customer Satisfaction

Satisfaction plateaued, and friction points were unclear. To address this, we deployed comprehensive user surveys across various website touchpoints, gathering both quantitative satisfaction scores and detailed qualitative feedback on areas like product information, checkout speed, and comparison tools.

Survey results directed us to enhance product pages with richer content, optimize the checkout flow further, and implement a dedicated product comparison feature. These targeted improvements addressed explicit pain points, significantly enhancing overall satisfaction and shopping efficiency.
